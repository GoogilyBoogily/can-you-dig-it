Print settings

The 3MF opens in Bambu Studio or Orca with every part already on a plate. Suggested settings: 2 walls, 6 % gyroid, 0.28 mm layers, PETG.

Every piece prints flat, face up, with nothing to support. A lane is a deck, two walls and an end wall that drop together on tabs; gangs lock with dovetails; no glue. Long lanes split into two keyed halves at the bed size. Source: can-you-dig-it.
