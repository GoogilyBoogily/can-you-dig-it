Assembly and printed-fit guidance

Assembly steps

Decks, walls and covers print flat, face up, without supports. Printed joints and keys need no glue or hardware.

  1. For split lanes, join the deck sections in order at each T joint. Slide the wall sections onto their hidden keys along the lane, working from one end.
  2. Snap the joined walls onto the deck ears. Drop the end wall into the side-wall slots and snap it onto the deck.
  3. Build each tier before adding the next. Join all cover sections on their keys before lowering the joined cover onto the wall pins. Support the loaded organizer underneath.

Support the bottom when moving a loaded dispenser; print the joint test pieces and qualify a complete lane before loading the full assembly.

Assembly steps for connected stacks

Decks, walls and covers print flat, face up, without supports. The joining keys between stacks print standing on their ends, up to 40 mm high.

  1. Assemble outside the shelf, without cans. For split parts, join the deck sections at every T joint and slide the wall sections onto their hidden keys before snapping the walls onto the decks.
  2. Join the bottom decks and walls. Feed each boundary’s key segments in from the dispensing front toward the closed rear stop.
  3. Check both retaining gates have returned behind the last segment, then add the upper tiers and joined covers. Support the loaded organizer underneath.

Support the bottom when moving a loaded organizer. Printed fit and loaded strength still need qualification; test the printed joints and a complete stack before loading the full organizer.
